Core Research Question:
When do autonomous systems with learned world models fail to predict reality accurately, and how can we detect and prevent that failure before it cascades into catastrophe?

The Problem

Robots and autonomous systems learn models of their environment to imagine futures and plan actions. These world models work—until they don't. The moment they compress too much uncertainty into overconfident predictions, imagination becomes hallucination, and safe planning becomes dangerous.

I'm building mathematical tools to predict exactly when and why this happens, and designing control policies that respect those limits.

Research Infrastructure

Live memory inspector for Transformers, SSMs, and RNNs. Watch hidden state drift during streaming inference.

Pre-training diagnostic that detects latent regime unidentifiability before training begins.
